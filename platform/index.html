The HOME Platform

The HOME platform consists of a patient app and a clinician dashboard that together enable seamless remote clinical monitoring.

Patient App

Co-designed with patients with lived experience of chronic obstructive pulmonary disorder (COPD), this app allows patients to log their symptom history in a daily diary. It also connects with wearable devices and commodity medical devices like Fitbits and blood pressure cuffs.

Clinician Dashboard

Co-designed with clinicians across disciplines, this dashboard allows healthcare providers to efficiently review data from monitored patients. The dashboard highlights abnormal values and provides data-driven alerts.
