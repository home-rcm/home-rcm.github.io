About HOME

Learn more about our team and our vision for healthcare innovation.

Who We Are

We are a transdisciplinary research team with expertise spanning clinical medicine, health system innovation, and machine learning, working alongside patients and caregivers to build tools that make remote care possible.

What is Our Vision

We foresee a future where personalized healthcare extends seamlessly into every home, empowering individuals with timely support to catch health changes early, prevent complications, and keep people out of the hospital.

Why Now

Canada's healthcare system faces a severe crisis driven by an aging population, rising rates of chronic disease, and limited primary care access that overcrowds emergency departments. Scalable, affordable solutions are urgently required to manage chronic conditions at home and prevent unnecessary hospital visits.

How Are We Different

Many commercial products are expensive and generic, while academic projects are inflexible. We are building a solution that will be configurable for various populations and health conditions, creating a virtuous cycle of evidence and innovation.
