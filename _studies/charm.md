title: "Remote Monitoring of COPD Patients at Home"
status: active
start_date: 2024-09
description: "This study is designed to support patients managing chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) at home. Patients were provided with a smartphone app to track their daily symptoms, a smartwatch to continuously monitor their vital signs and mobility, and an additional device to monitor their cough frequency. This data is regularly monitored by a practicing physician."
inclusion_criteria:
- Adults 18 years or older with a primary diagnosis of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
- Hospital discharge for a COPD exacerbation within the previous 30 days
- Ability to operate a compatible smartphone and wear a smartwatch continuously
