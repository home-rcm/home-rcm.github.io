About HOME

To improve health outcomes, patient experience, and population health, our team is developing, refining, and evaluating a connected remote monitoring platform that brings clinical-grade monitoring into patients' homes.

Who We Are

We are a multidisciplinary and transdisciplinary team with expertise spanning clinical research, health system innovation, remote monitoring systems, and machine learning — working alongside patients and caregivers to build tools that make remote care possible.

Our Vision

Remote clinical monitoring that is available and accessible to everyone, enabling early detection and timely intervention to prevent complications and reduce avoidable emergency visits and hospitalizations.

Our Mission

To co-develop, co-implement, co-evaluate, and co-scale a remote monitoring platform that benefits patients and clinicians alike — enabling earlier detection of health changes and better outcomes for people managing their health at home.

The HOME Platform

The HOME platform consists of a patient-facing app and a clinician dashboard that together enable seamless remote clinical monitoring.

For Patients

Patients can log daily symptoms and vital signs through a simple diary, while connected wearable devices collect additional health data such as heart rate, sleep, and activity — all from the comfort of home.

For Clinicians

The clinician dashboard synthesizes this information, helping healthcare providers identify early warning signs and intervene before conditions worsen — supporting patients to stay healthier at home.
