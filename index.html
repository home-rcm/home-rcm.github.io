Who We Are

(GENERAL HOME TEAM PITCH) We are a multidisciplinary and transdisciplinary team with expertise spanning clinical research, health system innovation, remote monitoring systems, and machine learning — working alongside patients and caregivers to build tools that make remote care possible.

Our Vision

Remote clinical monitoring that is available and accessible to everyone, enabling early detection and timely intervention to prevent complications and reduce avoidable emergency visits and hospitalizations.

Our Mission

To co-develop, co-implement, co-evaluate, and co-scale a remote monitoring platform that benefits patients and clinicians alike — enabling earlier detection of health changes and better outcomes for people managing their health at home.

In partnership with

The HOME Platform

The HOME platform consists of a patient-facing app and a clinician dashboard that together enable seamless remote clinical monitoring.
